Overview

Synthesised from 71 reviews | Last Published September 9, 2026

HubSpot CRM is primarily utilized by organizations to manage customer relationships, address fragmented data, and streamline sales and marketing processes. In TrustRadius reviews, it is widely adopted for contact and lead management, enabling users to monitor leads, organize follow-ups, and serve as a centralized "single source of truth" for customer information across departments. Its capabilities extend to sales pipeline management, with 38% of reviewers highlighting its effectiveness in contact and lead management.

Reviewers also appreciate the overall time-saving benefits and improved data visibility. However, common drawbacks include concerns about reporting flexibility, especially for advanced features, and the overall pricing structure, with 14% citing issues with paid features. Despite these cost and feature limitations, the prevailing sentiment suggests a positive impact on operational effectiveness and revenue generation.

Pros

  • Automates sales and marketing workflows effectively
  • Centralizes contact and customer data into a single source
  • Provides seamless integrations with essential business tools
  • Offers robust reporting and customizable dashboards for insights
  • Contributes to significant time savings through process efficiencies

Cons

  • Limited flexibility and depth in reporting features, especially advanced ones
  • Higher-cost tiers often required for essential advanced functionalities
  • Overall pricing structure can become expensive with increased users/features
  • Mixed feedback on the robustness and breadth of third-party integrations
  • Navigation and platform complexity can be challenging for some users
