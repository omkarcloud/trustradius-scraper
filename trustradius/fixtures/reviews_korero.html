A CRM and Recruitment platform that made recruiting more rewarding

Rating: 9 out of 10
April 19, 2021

Use Cases and Deployment Scope

Korero has been a very efficient platform for my business. I am a freelance professional and to manage different clients and their recruitment cycles was a big hassle but ever since I started using Korero life became pretty organized. I could work on this platform with other recruiters and share my data with ease. Korero also has a CRM capability which helps me to keep track of business. It is very user friendly and the software has customization possible for individual needs. The best part is the affordability of Korero and I’m pretty pleased with their price and offerings. It is worth a spend for the benefits it accrues and is very reasonably priced as compared to other software in the market.

Pros

  • Customization of fields as per individual needs
  • Reasonably priced
  • Sales and new business analysis can be well tapped
  • Recruiting software works amazing as all small details are very well incorporated

Cons

  • Social media integration is needed
  • There is room for integration with many HR applications to make it more beneficial for HR professionals
  • Payroll integration is something I missed but got to know it’s on the cards, so coming soon probably

Likelihood to Recommend

Korero would be the best choice for freelance professionals, small and medium size companies, in my opinion.
It is also a great platform for those who are looking for ATS and CRM together at an affordable price.
For a one man company it becomes expensive to buy software and manage relationships and recruitment process online and in that scenario Korero platform comes handy with its offerings and price. Its relatively new in the market so I would say they can bring more add-ons with time but for now I feel it has a lot of potential to support the recruiters like me and many other businesses.
